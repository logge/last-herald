The Last Herald

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

In short: The Last Herald collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your saved games stay on your own device.

The Last Herald is a free point-and-click adventure game made by Peter Logarn. It's for Android, on Google Play, and for the browser, on itch.io, and is coming to iPhone and iPad on the App Store. This policy covers the game wherever you play it, and this website.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information or any other data about you or how you play. It has:

  • no accounts or sign-in;
  • no advertising;
  • no analytics, tracking or crash reporting;
  • no in-app purchases;
  • no chat or other ways to contact other players.

What the game keeps on your device

To let you carry on where you left off, the game saves on your own device:

  • your saved games (the automatic save and up to five save slots);
  • your settings: whether the sound is on, and which language the text is shown in.

These stay on your device and are never sent anywhere. In the apps they're kept in the app's own storage, and deleting the app (or, on Android, clearing its storage in the settings) deletes them. In the browser they're kept in the browser's storage for the game's page, and clearing that site's data deletes them.

The first time it starts, the game checks your device's language setting, on the device, to pick the language of the text. That isn't stored anywhere except as the setting above.

The internet

The apps don't use the internet: everything the game needs (the pictures, the music and the voices) is inside the app, and it makes no connections to any server. In the browser, the game's files are downloaded from itch.io when you play, and nothing is sent back.

Other companies

The game has no code from advertising, analytics or any other third-party service.

The stores you get the game from have privacy policies of their own, covering what they collect when you use their services:

  • Google (Google Play): policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple (the App Store): apple.com/legal/privacy
  • itch.io: itch.io/docs/legal/privacy-policy

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking, and loads nothing from any other site: its fonts and pictures are its own. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and like any web host, GitHub may record technical details such as your IP address when you visit, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The game is not aimed at children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because it doesn't collect information at all.

Changes

If the game ever changes in a way that affects this policy, the policy will be updated on this page, with a new date at the top.
