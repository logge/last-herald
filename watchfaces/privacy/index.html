Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

In short: Sunward, Walkies and Roomie collect nothing. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. The one thing that ever leaves your phone is in Roomie's phone app, and only if you turn its weather on: a place, rounded to about a kilometre, sent to Open-Meteo for the weather there.

Sunward, Walkies and Roomie are watch faces for Wear OS watches, made by Peter Logarn and published on Google Play. This policy covers all three, and Roomie's phone app.

What the watch faces collect

Nothing. The watch faces do not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information or any other data about you. They have:

  • no accounts or sign-in;
  • no advertising;
  • no analytics, tracking or crash reporting;
  • no in-app purchases.

What they show

To draw a face, your watch's own watch face system gives it values the watch already has: the time and date, your heart rate, your steps and step goal, the weather, how many notifications you haven't read, and the battery level; and, in Sunward, the complications you choose for it. The faces only show these values on your watch's screen.

The faces have no code of their own: each is made only of pictures and a description of the face in Google's Watch Face Format, which your watch draws. So they can't read, keep or send anything anywhere, and they don't use the internet.

The settings you choose for a face (such as your wake-up time and bedtime) are kept by your watch, with the face, and never leave it.

Roomie's phone app

Roomie also has an app for your phone (on Google Play, Robin & Coco), to watch Robin on her island, have it as your wallpaper and put the face on your watch. It collects nothing about you. It keeps its own settings (such as whether Robin talks) on your phone. To find your watch, it asks your watch's own app on the phone, through Google Play services, which watches are paired; to put the face on the watch, it asks that app to open the Play Store there. Your watch's name stays on your phone.

The app can also show the weather on Robin's island. It's off until you turn it on, and you choose where it's from: a town you type in, or your phone's approximate location (Android asks your permission first, and the app asks for it only while it's open, never in the background). Either way the place is rounded to about a kilometre before it's kept or sent. The app then sends that place, and nothing else about you, to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com), a weather service, for its forecast: at most about twice an hour, and only while the app, or Robin's island as your wallpaper, is on your screen. A town you type is sent to Open-Meteo's place search, to find it. Like any web service, Open-Meteo sees your phone's IP address with each request; its terms say its logs, which may contain the place, are deleted after 90 days. The place and the forecast are kept only on your phone, and left out of its backups. Turn the weather off in the app's settings and nothing more is sent. The app uses the internet only for this: the link to this page opens in your browser.

Other companies

Neither the watch faces nor the phone app has any code for advertising, analytics or tracking.

Your heart rate, steps and weather come from your watch and the apps and services on it, under their own privacy policies; the faces only show them. Google Play, where you get the faces, has a privacy policy of its own: policies.google.com/privacy.

Open-Meteo, which the phone app asks for the weather if you turn it on, has terms of its own, with what it keeps: open-meteo.com/en/terms.

This page

This page has no cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking, and loads nothing from any other site. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and like any web host, GitHub may record technical details such as your IP address when you visit, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The watch faces aren't made for children in particular. They collect no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If the watch faces ever change in a way that affects this policy, the policy will be updated on this page, with a new date at the top.
