Watch faces by Peter Logarn

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

In short: Sunward, Walkies and Roomie collect nothing and send nothing anywhere. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Sunward, Walkies and Roomie are watch faces for Wear OS watches, made by Peter Logarn and published on Google Play. This policy covers all three.

What the watch faces collect

Nothing. The watch faces do not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information or any other data about you. They have:

  • no accounts or sign-in;
  • no advertising;
  • no analytics, tracking or crash reporting;
  • no in-app purchases.

What they show

To draw a face, your watch's own watch face system gives it values the watch already has: the time and date, your heart rate, your steps and step goal, the weather, how many notifications you haven't read, and the battery level; and, in Sunward, the complications you choose for it. The faces only show these values on your watch's screen.

The faces have no code of their own: each is made only of pictures and a description of the face in Google's Watch Face Format, which your watch draws. So they can't read, keep or send anything anywhere, and they don't use the internet.

The settings you choose for a face (such as your wake-up time and bedtime) are kept by your watch, with the face, and never leave it.

Other companies

The watch faces have no code from advertising, analytics or any other third-party service.

Your heart rate, steps and weather come from your watch and the apps and services on it, under their own privacy policies; the faces only show them. Google Play, where you get the faces, has a privacy policy of its own: policies.google.com/privacy.

This page

This page has no cookies, analytics, advertising or tracking, and loads nothing from any other site. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, and like any web host, GitHub may record technical details such as your IP address when you visit, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The watch faces aren't made for children in particular. They collect no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If the watch faces ever change in a way that affects this policy, the policy will be updated on this page, with a new date at the top.
